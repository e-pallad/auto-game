How to play

  1. You slide until a rock, water, or the edge stops you.
  2. The floe you're standing on is cracked: it melts when you leave, becoming water you'll bump into later.
  3. Pass over every ember, then stop on the dock. The dock stops slides and never melts.
  4. Match the solver's par. Stuck? Undo or Restart anytime.

Keys: arrows / WASD slide · Z or Backspace undo · R restart · Esc menu. Touch: swipe, tap a side of the board, or use the pad.
